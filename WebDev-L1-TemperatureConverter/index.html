°C

Celsius

Water freezes at 0° and boils at 100° at standard pressure. Common in everyday life around the world.

°F

Fahrenheit

Water freezes at 32° and boils at 212°. Often used for weather and cooking in the United States.

K

Kelvin

An absolute temperature scale used in science. Its zero point is absolute zero: −273.15°C.

∑

Every conversion starts by finding the same point on the Kelvin scale. Values below absolute zero are not physically possible.
